Chat workspace
YOU
But Complex II can still transfer electrons to ubiquinone. Why would oxygen consumption decrease if electrons can still enter the chain through Complex II?
AI ASSISTANT
Complex II can still reduce ubiquinone, but electrons from both Complex I and Complex II converge at the ubiquinone pool. They must still pass through Complex III to reach cytochrome c and Complex IV. When Complex III is inhibited, downstream electron flow is restricted, so oxygen reduction decreases.
YOU
So Complex II does not bypass Complex III. Both pathways converge at ubiquinone. Is that the correct interpretation?
AI ASSISTANT
Yes. Complex II is an alternative entry point for electrons, not an alternative route around Complex III.

Two-layer multi-agent execution

A lightweight synchronous capture layer preserves the normal chatbot experience; specialist analysis runs asynchronously in the background.

Layer 1 · Synchronous capture

ACTIVE DURING CHAT
Agent 1 · Evidence Capture

Records student events in real time: draft, prompts, revisions, final answer and reflection, with timestamps and provenance. This capture layer is designed not to add analytical processing to chatbot response time.

Layer 2 · Asynchronous specialist analysis

EVENT-TRIGGERED

Agents 2–8 are activated selectively when relevant evidence arrives or a submission milestone is reached. Analysis is consolidated to avoid repeatedly processing unchanged content.

Agent Orchestrator

Planned validation

  • Expert-annotated student interaction dataset.
  • Agreement between agent evidence classifications and lecturer judgements.
  • Precision, recall, false-positive and false-negative analysis.
  • Calibration, traceability and appropriate abstention when evidence is insufficient.
  • Separate evaluation of changes in student D–J–O performance over time.

Three-tier source verification

EVIDENCE STATUS
LevelMeaningExample in this record
1 · ReportedStudent states that a source was checked.Student reports checking lecture notes.
2 · DocumentedStudent supplies an identifiable source, citation or passage.Lecture notes are named, but no page or passage is attached.
3 · AuthenticatedSystem or lecturer inspects the source directly and confirms whether it supports the claim.Not established in this demonstration.

Semantic similarity can help locate relevant passages, but similarity alone does not establish that a source supports a claim. Level 1 is reported, Level 2 is documented, and Level 3 is authenticated.

Assessment governance

  • Do not infer critical thinking from prompt count, chat length or a single challenge.
  • One item of evidence may inform more than one criterion, but must not be double-counted as independent evidence.
  • Distinguish student-reported verification from authenticated source checking.
  • Use “not observed in this captured record” where evidence is missing.
  • Lecturers can accept, modify or override provisional scores and provide formative feedback.
